Before you put AI in front of parents.

Does Wave AI replace my admin team?

No. It handles repetitive early-stage questions and prepares better context. Your team controls sensitive decisions, final fees and important relationships.

Can it work in Chinese and English?

Yes. The conversation follows the language parents naturally use, including mixed-language messages.

Can it connect to our calendar or CRM?

Often, yes. We first verify whether the tool offers a secure API, webhook or supported integration, then define what the Agent may read or change.

How much does it cost?

It depends on channels, workflow depth and integrations. The Agent can understand your scope first; the Wave AI team confirms any final quotation.
