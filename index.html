06 / FAQ

Before you put AI in front of customers.

Does ASAP replace my sales team?

No. It handles repetitive early-stage work and prepares better context. Your team controls sensitive decisions, final pricing and important relationships.

Can it work in Chinese and English?

Yes. The conversation can follow the language your customers naturally use, including mixed-language messages.

Can it connect to our calendar or CRM?

Often, yes. We first verify whether the tool offers a secure API, webhook or supported integration, then define what the Agent may read or change.

How much does it cost?

It depends on channels, workflow depth and integrations. The Agent can understand your scope first; the ASAP team confirms any final quotation.
