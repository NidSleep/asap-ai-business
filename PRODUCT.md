# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of small and medium businesses in Malaysia, in any industry, who sell through chat apps. Many enquiries arrive on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram, often after hours, and the team cannot reply fast enough or keep following up. They read in English or Chinese (both are supported; the page follows the visitor's browser language).

They meet Wave AI on its landing page while deciding whether to try an AI sales system. Their job on the page is to understand what it does differently from an ordinary chatbot, judge whether it fits their business and budget, and book a short Zoom demo.

## Product Purpose

Wave AI is an AI sales system for businesses: not only a customer-service chatbot, but an AI that sells. It has two parts:

- **AI front-line salesperson**: replies within 30 seconds, 24/7; works out what the customer needs; handles objections; follows up automatically on days 1, 3, 5 and 7; and pushes for the sale or a booking.
- **AI back-office manager**: reviews chat history, finds where and why leads were lost, and produces reports.

Success for the landing page is a booked Zoom demo.

## Positioning

Wave AI frames chatbots as a four-level ladder and sells the top two levels:

1. Traditional bot: fixed menus and keywords.
2. Basic AI Q&A: answers questions, then stops.
3. AI salesperson: proactive follow-up, objection handling, closing or booking.
4. AI sales manager: reviews conversations, explains lost leads, reports.

Most chatbots stop at level 1 or 2; Wave AI works at levels 3 and 4. The core line: "AI customer service answers when someone asks; an AI sales team keeps pushing until the deal closes."

## Operating Context

- Channels: WhatsApp (including the WhatsApp Business API), Messenger and Instagram.
- Setup material the client's customers provide: real past sales chats (to learn their best salespeople's wording), product PDF brochures, the company website, and refund / after-sales SOPs.
- Demo booking: the AI sends a Calendly link in chat; the prospect picks a time and receives a Zoom invite with no manual scheduling.
- Chat style of the AI: short messages sent one at a time like a person typing, never a wall of text; professional, warm and guiding (sales-consultant tone); light emoji such as 🚀 📅 👋; offers a benefit, case or limited-time offer when the customer hesitates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Plans (reference prices from the client, final quote confirmed by their team):
  - DIY (自助版): about RM 500 / month; basic AI salesperson, a credit allowance, self-service setup.
  - Guided Launch (指导启动版): about RM 6,000 / year; one-to-one expert setup, testing and tuning, team training.
  - Enterprise (企业定制版): quote on request; multi-department collaboration, complex workflows, deep CRM integration.
- Standard answers the client wants used:
  - AI does not replace the sales team; it handles screening, follow-up and questions so people focus on high-intent customers and complex negotiation.
  - The AI's tone is natural and warm; it sends short split messages and follows context.
  - It connects to existing WhatsApp via the WhatsApp Business API and other major platforms.
- The site is bilingual (English / 中文). Language switching is a low-key footer control, not a header feature.
- Undecided:
  - Calendly link: "Book a demo" falls back to the client's Telegram Agent until it arrives.
  - Domain: an existing company subdomain (e.g. ai.<company>.com) or a new domain.

## Brand Commitments

- This is a client project. The developer builds the site; the client owns product facts, claims, pricing and final copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Claims supplied by the client but **not yet backed by customer data**: 85% lead-diagnosis accuracy, 80% less manual work, 2× bookings for an aesthetics clinic. Treat them as unverified claims, not proven results; do not present them as real customer outcomes until the client supplies the data.
- Promised by the client, not yet received: a 30–60 s product demo recording (customer's WhatsApp on one side, the AI replying and reading intent on the other), a screenshot of the AI manager's dashboard, and the aesthetics-clinic case study.
- No testimonials, customer logos or named customers exist yet. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. **Sell the difference, not the chatbot.** Every surface should make the jump from level 1–2 to level 3–4 obvious.
2. **Show the conversation.** The product is a chat; real-feeling message flows prove it better than adjectives.
3. **Claims need proof.** Numbers and cases appear as proven only with the client's evidence; until then, keep them out or clearly mark them.
4. **One action.** Booking a Zoom demo is the goal; everything else supports it.
5. **Two languages, one product.** English and Chinese are equal; neither is a translation afterthought.
