Pain cards, 2x2 feature grid, stat band, three-column pricing, accordion and glow-box finale are category templates; below Levels the page stops showing the conversation (PRODUCT.md Principle 2).

## Priority Issues
- [P0] Unverified 85%/80%/2x shown under "Results from our customers / 客户的真实成果" with ticker animation, plus a visible internal placeholder box ("Space for the aesthetics clinic case..."). Violates PRODUCT.md evidence rules. Fix: remove or reframe as targets with explicit label, no ticker; delete placeholder box from production. Command: clarify.
- [P1] CTA promises Zoom demo booking but every "Book a demo" opens t.me/presale_copyabc_bot (lib/content.ts DEMO_URL). Fix: ship Calendly link; meanwhile label honestly and add a WhatsApp option. Command: harden.
- [P1] Pricing hard to compare (RM 500/month vs RM 6,000/year = same monthly; unclear if Guided includes or adds), "credits" unquantified, DIY "Set it up yourself" still forces "Book a demo", no term/cancellation/time-to-live reassurance (must come from client). Command: clarify.
- [P2] Contrast and tap targets: h1 second line navy/45 = 2.9:1 (needs 3:1 large) and weaker than the pain line; "Illustrative screen" 11px white/40 on navy = 3.7:1; header CTA 36px tall; footer language buttons 21px tall (below 24px minimum). Command: audit, then polish.
- [P2] Middle of page drops the product's visual language: generic icon-tile pain cards, Features restates Levels, glow-box finale. Fix: merge Features into Levels; Pain as micro chat scenes; finale continues the hero conversation. Command: distill, then bolder.
